# Institutional constraints for model v1

These are implementation design interpretations of constitutional and agency sources, not a complete statement of current Ghanaian law. Numeric vote odds, institutional scores, policy delays, resistance and election outcomes are all game assumptions. Fictional personalities and the inauguration calendar must be labelled.

| Constraint | Source rule | Required game behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Presidency | [Article 66](https://judicial.gov.gh/index.php/the-executive): four-year term, maximum two elected terms | Sixteen quarterly turns per full term; defeat ends presidential control; legacy follows. |
| Legislative route | [Articles 106, 108](https://judicial.gov.gh/index.php/the-legislature): parliamentary bills and assent; specified financial initiatives introduced by/on behalf of President | Tax/new-law proposals may fail in Parliament. Announcing a proposal cannot override rejection. |
| Taxes and public money | [Articles 174, 178–181](https://judicial.gov.gh/index.php/finance): legal taxation, appropriation/supplementary authority and parliamentary loan controls | Funding and legal authority are separate gates; borrowing is not revenue; spent cash and incurred obligations must enter the ledger. |
| Courts | [Articles 125–127](https://judicial.gov.gh/index.php/the-judiciary): independent judicial power | A presidential slider cannot direct judicial verdicts. Court investment changes capacity through time; legal review can constrain action. |
| Procurement | [Act 663 as amended](https://ppa.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Public-Procurement-Amendment-Act-2016-ACT663_RePrinted.pdf) | Procurement, staffing and oversight precede implemented output. No new specific monetary thresholds are asserted in v1. |
| Land | [Constitution land chapter](https://judicial.gov.gh/index.php/land-and-natural-resources) distinguishes public and customary interests | Farm participation, rights and compensation matter; the player does not own every cocoa farm. |
| Monetary policy | [BoG framework](https://www.bog.gov.gh/monetary-policy/our-monetary-policy-framework/): MPC sets instruments for price stability | Simulated MPC response separate from presidential actions; reserves separate from treasury. The response formula is an assumption. |
| Audit | [Article 187](https://judicial.gov.gh/index.php/finance): independent public audit and reporting | Funding audit capacity does not guarantee all funds are recovered; findings can trigger scrutiny and resistance. |

The UI should display a short legal-route string and source links for each policy. Recommended route groups:

- Farmer pricing: PPRC/COCOBOD decision and financing; parliamentary budget authority where public funding is required.
- Rehabilitation, extension, storage and irrigation: implementing agency, farmer/land participation, appropriation, procurement and maintenance.
- Land administration: responsible land institutions and rights-holder processes; legislation for changed legal rights.
- SME guarantees: financial approvals, eligibility, lender participation, contingent-liability provision; losses recognised when guarantees are called.
- Licensing and formalisation: responsible regulator and existing powers; Parliament if changing primary legislation.
- Competition: legislation and independent enforcement institutions where legal changes are required; do not claim a particular new competition Act was verified.
- Processing and export investment: participating businesses, appropriations for public support, utilities, procurement/permits and feedstock; preserve private ownership.
- Roads/ports, grid, generation: responsible agencies/utilities, contracts, land, approvals and recurring maintenance; fiscal cost is not identical to national project value.
- Energy support and cash transfers: targeted eligibility, funded recurrent transfers, delivery capacity and audit.
- Health, schools, technical training: ministries/local providers, staffing, procurement, enrolment or take-up and recurrent costs.
- Tax/compliance, spending reallocation and debt: finance ministry plus parliamentary legal/appropriation/borrowing authority as required; cannot abolish obligations by declaring a policy.
- Mining licensing/royalties: rights, permits, environmental safeguards and statutory fiscal route; state royalty income is a portion of value, not all exports.
- Procurement/audit, merit administration and court capacity: relevant independent/professional institutions, appropriations and lawful processes; capacity improvement incurs costs and delays.
- Local accountability/whistleblowing: competent bodies, safeguards, legal changes if needed and credible implementation; no direct automatic productivity bonus.

Policy authority is intentionally simplified. A court injunction, legislative rejection or implementation failure should name its mechanism as a fictional model event, not as a report that a Ghanaian institution actually acted.
